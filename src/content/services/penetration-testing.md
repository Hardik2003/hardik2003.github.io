We conduct adversary-emulation testing for web apps, APIs, networks, and cloud environments using MITRE ATT&CK techniques, and provide proof of concepts, CWE/CVE mappings, and retest validation.

## Testing the way an attacker would

An automated scanner finds what it has a signature for. A real attacker chains three low-severity issues into account takeover. We test the way the second one works — manually, with tooling as an assistant rather than the method.

Engagements are scoped against MITRE ATT&CK so coverage is explicit and comparable between tests. You know which techniques were attempted, which succeeded, and which were blocked by controls that worked.

## Proof, not speculation

Every finding ships with a working proof of concept: the exact request, the resulting response, and the reproduction steps. Nothing is reported as "potentially vulnerable". If we could not demonstrate it, it goes in the observations section, clearly separated from confirmed findings.

Findings are mapped to CWE and, where applicable, CVE, so they slot into your existing tracking rather than living in a separate document.

## Retest included

A test that ends at the report leaves the important question open. After you remediate, we retest the findings and issue updated validation confirming what is genuinely closed — which is what your customers and auditors are actually asking for.
