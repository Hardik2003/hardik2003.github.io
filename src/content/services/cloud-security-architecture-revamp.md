Seamlessly migrate from on-premise to cloud, architecting secure, automated, and highly available environments tailored to your needs.

A lift-and-shift migration moves your problems into someone else's data centre. We rebuild the foundation so the platform you land on is genuinely better than the one you left.

## Architecture first

We begin with a thorough audit of your existing infrastructure — on-premise or cloud — and design a target architecture that accounts for the workloads you run today and the ones you expect in two years. That means a proper landing zone: account separation, network segmentation, centralised logging, and guardrails that make the insecure option the difficult one.

Identity is where most cloud breaches begin, so access governance is designed in rather than added later. We implement least-privilege IAM, short-lived credentials, and a secrets management approach that removes long-lived keys from your codebase and CI system entirely.

## Availability and cost

Highly available does not have to mean expensive. We design for the availability target your business actually needs, then use right-sizing, automated scheduling and usage tracking to keep the bill proportionate. Cost visibility is built in from the start — budget alerts and per-team attribution, so a runaway workload surfaces in hours rather than at month end.

Every environment we build is described in code, so it can be rebuilt, reviewed and audited. Nothing depends on a configuration that exists only in one console.
