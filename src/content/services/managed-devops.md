Most companies our size cannot justify hiring a cloud architect, an SRE and a security engineer. They hire one generalist, load them with everything, and the platform gets attention only when something breaks.

A retainer solves the same problem differently: an engineering team that already knows your environment, available continuously, at a fraction of three salaries.

## What it covers

Monitoring and incident response, with on-call cover matched to how critical your platform is. Patching and version upgrades handled on a schedule instead of deferred until a CVE forces an emergency. Monthly cost review, so spend gets examined regularly rather than once a year when someone notices the bill.

Alongside the reactive work there is a continuous improvement backlog - the reliability, automation and cost items that never reach the top of a product roadmap but compound significantly over a year.

## How we work

You get named engineers, not a ticket queue. The same people, who have your architecture in their heads and can tell you why a decision was made eighteen months ago.

Everything we build is documented and in your repositories. Nothing depends on us being available, and there is no lock-in dressed up as a service - if you bring the capability in-house later, the handover is a conversation rather than an excavation.

## Sizing it

Retainers are scoped to your platform and how much cover you need. A stable environment with business-hours support looks very different from a payments platform with 24x7 on-call. We size against what you actually run rather than selling a tier.
