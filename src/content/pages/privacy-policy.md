At ScionTech we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, interact with our services, or make use of our products.

## Information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us when you interact with our website, including but not limited to:

- **Personally identifiable information (PII)** such as your name, email address, phone number, and company name.
- **Non-personally identifiable information** such as browser type, operating system, pages visited on our site, and time spent on each page.

## How we use your information

The information we collect may be used in the following ways:

- To provide and improve our services
- To personalise your experience on our website
- To respond to enquiries and customer service requests
- To send you updates or newsletters, where you have opted in

## Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and Google Analytics to understand usage patterns on our site and improve the experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may limit some functionality.

## How we protect your information

We implement a range of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and secure hosting.

## Sharing your information

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except in the following cases:

- **Service providers.** We may share information with trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating our website or providing services to you, such as our form and analytics providers.
- **Legal compliance.** We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or to protect our rights.

## Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. These sites have separate and independent privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Access, update, or request deletion of your personal information
- Opt out of marketing communications using the unsubscribe link in any email
- Withdraw your consent to the collection of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

## Children's privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child, we will take steps to delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page and the effective date updated accordingly.
