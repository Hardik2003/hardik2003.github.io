We secured Eazybot against frequent DDoS-driven outages by integrating a CDN with WAF rules, cutting attack traffic by over 70%.

To eliminate storage bottlenecks, we resized disks and boosted IOPS headroom, preventing I/O failures during peak loads. An ELK-based monitoring stack was added to detect and alert on suspicious traffic patterns in real time.

We then architected cross-region database geo-replication and moved critical services into private subnets, lifting platform availability to 99.95%. This comprehensive overhaul restored user trust, directly fuelling a 25% increase in trading volume.
